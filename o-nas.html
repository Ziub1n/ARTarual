about me

My name is Laura and I make rings. No team, no catalogue, no warehouse — just a bench, a set of tools and a head full of shapes I have not seen in any shop.

from one ring

I made the first ring because I could not find anything I actually wanted to wear. Friends started asking where I had bought it. The answer — “I made it myself” — came up often enough that it eventually turned into a studio.

Today I design and forge everything myself, in a small studio in Warsaw — most often from reclaimed metals, and always in short runs.

short history

back then

then

My own studio in Warsaw and the first signets somebody other than me wanted to wear.

now

Over 600 rings made, custom signets and engraving, and still the same single bench.
