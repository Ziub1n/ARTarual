about me

Hi, I’m Laura. I make ARTarual — a jewellery brand for modern princesses. Not the fairy-tale kind, though.

modern princess

To me, a princess is a woman with her own plans — who works, tries to keep everyday life together, sometimes has no idea what she is doing, and keeps going anyway.

I wanted to shift what that word means. To take the spell off it and build my own version of the Modern Princess — strong, independent, and above all, real.

see what I make

how it started

self‑taught

Jewellery has always been more than an accessory to me. I taught myself goldsmithing — through trial, error and a curiosity that never really stopped.

then

Eventually I went a step further and completed postgraduate studies at the Academy of Fine Arts in Łódź.

now

Today I make every ARTarual piece with my own hands — from the first idea, through carving jeweller’s wax and working the silver, to the final polish.
