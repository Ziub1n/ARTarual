size guide

You do not need to go anywhere or buy anything. A strip of paper, a ruler and a quiet minute will do — and if you are still unsure, just write to me and we will work it out.

three steps

01

wrap your finger in paper

Cut a strip about a centimetre wide and wrap it around the base of your finger — where the ring is meant to sit. Snug, but not cutting in.

02

mark it and measure

Mark where the strip meets itself, unroll it and hold it against a ruler. That number in millimetres is your finger circumference.

03

subtract 40

Circumference minus 40 is the size I work in. 53 mm becomes size 13. The table below has all of it ready for you.

before you choose

evening

Measure at the end of the day, at normal room temperature. Fingers are noticeably thinner in the morning and in the cold, and a ring sized then may not go on later.

wide bands

Wide rings (6 mm and up) sit tighter than narrow ones. If you are between two sizes, take the larger one for a wide band.

knuckle

The ring has to pass the knuckle. If your joints are more pronounced, measure both at the base and at the knuckle and I will pick something in between.

got it wrong

It happens. I resize once free of charge — you only pay postage one way.
