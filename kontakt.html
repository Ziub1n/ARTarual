frequent questions

Will you make it in my size?
Yes, everything is made to measure — sizes 8 to 24. If you do not know yours, go to the size guide and measure it with a strip of paper in two minutes.
How long is the wait?
Two to five weeks depending on the piece and the material. Solid gold and ordered-in stones add a couple of weeks. You see the exact lead time on every ring page.
Can I change the stone colour or the metal?
The stone colour, yes — pick from what is shown on the ring page and the price updates as you go. The metal itself is fixed to sterling silver 930 for pieces in the drop; if you want gold, that becomes a custom order.
Is there a bag?
There is — you can collect several rings in it, but there is no payment on the site. The bag turns into one email to me, and I confirm everything personally before I start.
