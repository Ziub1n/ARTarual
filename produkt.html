before you order

How do I know my size?
The easiest way is to measure the circumference of your finger with a strip of paper — I wrote it up step by step in the size guide. If you are between two sizes, take the larger one: wide bands sit tighter.
Why does the price go up with a bigger size?
A bigger ring is simply more silver or gold. The surcharge is shown on every size tile, so nothing surprises you at the end.
Can I get a stone colour that is not on the list?
Often yes — write to me and I will check what I can get. Stones ordered in specially add about a week to the lead time.
What if it does not fit?
I resize once free of charge, you only pay postage one way. Details on the shipping and returns page.
How do I look after the silver?
Wear it. Silver darkens with time and that is the point — if you want the shine back, a soft cloth is enough. Oxidised pieces will wear back to silver on the raised parts, which is how they are meant to age.
