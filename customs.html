customs

Sometimes the thing in your head does not exist in any collection — mine included. That is when we build it from zero: from a conversation and a sketch, through wax, to a finished piece in your size.

from idea to ring

01

you tell me what is in your head

A shape, a colour, a photo from Pinterest, an old ring from your grandmother — anything. You do not need to name it, that is my job.

02

sketch and quote

I send back a few drawings and a firm price. Nothing starts until you say “that is the one”. Changes at this stage are free.

03

wax, metal, your hand

I carve it in wax, cast it and finish it. You get photos along the way, and at the end a ring nobody else has.

custom questions

How much does a custom cost?
It depends on the metal, the stone and how much carving is involved. Simple signets start around the price of a collection piece; solid gold is a different conversation. You always get a firm number before anything begins.
How long does it take?
Six weeks and up, counted from the moment we agree on the sketch. Solid gold and ordered-in stones add a couple of weeks.
Can you engrave something inside?
Yes — an initial, a date, a short word. Tell me in the order what it should say and where.
